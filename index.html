TrueScope is an independent YouTube channel about Grand Theft Auto VI. Its short videos show what Rockstar Games' own official material and named reporting actually say, with the source on screen and a label for how certain each claim is.

Watch: youtube.com/@truescopegta

About the TrueScope Uploader app

“TrueScope Uploader” is a private tool used only by the owner of the TrueScope YouTube channel. Through Google’s sign-in screen and the YouTube APIs, it lets the owner upload and schedule the channel’s own videos, edit their titles and descriptions, and read the channel’s own statistics (such as views and watch time) to plan better videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What the app can access, how that data is used and stored, and how to revoke access are explained in the privacy policy.
